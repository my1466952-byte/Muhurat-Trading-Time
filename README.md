## Diwali Muhurat Trading 2026 Date and Time

In 2026, Diwali Muhurat Trading will take place on **Sunday, 8 November 2026**, marking the beginning of Samvat 2083.

The NSE and BSE will conduct a special trading session with the following timings:

| Trading Session | Time (IST) |
|---|---|
| Pre-Opening Session | 2:05 PM – 2:20 PM |
| Muhurat Trading Session | 2:20 PM – 3:30 PM |

Unlike regular market hours, this special session provides investors with a limited opportunity to buy and sell securities during Diwali. Many investors consider it an auspicious time to begin new investments.

## Why Is Muhurat Trading Important?

Muhurat Trading represents prosperity, positive beginnings and financial planning. Investors traditionally make symbolic purchases during this session.

However, market movements still depend on company performance, demand, supply and broader economic conditions. Therefore, investors should research stocks before making decisions.
